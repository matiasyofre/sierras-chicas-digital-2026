## When to Apply

Use this Skill when the task involves **UI structure, visual design decisions, interaction patterns, or user experience quality control**: designing new pages, creating/refactoring UI components, choosing color/typography/spacing/layout systems, reviewing UI for UX/accessibility/consistency, implementing navigation/animation/responsive behavior, or improving perceived quality and usability.

## Design Intelligence & Rules for High-Converting Portals & Marketplaces

1. **Color Harmony & Tonal Depth**:
   - Avoid generic flat grays (`#e2e8f0` everywhere) and harsh plain borders.
   - Use curated regional palettes: Deep Slate/Navy (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`), Warm Amber/Gold (`#f59e0b`, `#fbbf24`), Forest Emerald (`#059669`, `#10b981`), and Terracotta tints.
   - Employ smooth background shifts with ultra-subtle radial lighting.

2. **Typography & Hierarchy**:
   - Display fonts: Bold, modern, high-contrast headings (`font-black tracking-tight`).
   - Body typography: Highly readable, generous line-height (`leading-relaxed`), clear distinction between secondary and primary text.

3. **Bento Grid & Elevated Surface Physics**:
   - Card radius: Smooth, generous rounding (`rounded-3xl` / `rounded-[2rem]`).
   - Shadows: Diffused multi-layer ambient glows (`shadow-xl shadow-slate-900/5` to `shadow-2xl`).
   - Borderless elegance: Rely on tonal contrast, soft elevations, and background tints instead of rigid 1px grey lines.

4. **Interaction & Micro-animations**:
   - Responsive hover feedback with `transform active:scale-[0.98]` and smooth color transitions.
   - Live status beacons (pulsing green/amber dots for open businesses and online attention).
   - High-contrast primary CTA buttons with gradient accents and tactile elevation.
